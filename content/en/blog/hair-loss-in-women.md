> **In brief:**
> 1. Up to 40% of women experience diffuse hair thinning after menopause.
> 2. Biotin, zinc, iron and vitamin D show a measurable effect on hair health when a deficiency is diagnosed.
> 3. Visible results usually take 3 to 6 months, given the 2- to 6-year hair growth cycle.

## Understanding hair loss in women

Hair loss isn't only a men's issue. In women, diffuse hair loss usually shows up as overall thinning rather than a localized bald patch, with hormonal shifts as the main driver. Understanding the underlying mechanisms is the first step toward an effective solution.

### Prevalence by life stage

The share of women affected rises significantly with age, particularly around menopause, when hormonal changes affect the hair cycle.

## Science-backed causes

Several factors contribute to hair loss in women at the same time, which is why looking at just one in isolation rarely works.

### Most common triggers

- Hormonal changes (menopause, pregnancy, thyroid)
- Iron deficiency, affecting up to 20% of women of childbearing age according to studies
- Chronic stress, which shortens the growth phase of the hair cycle

## Nutrients with proven effects

| Nutrient | Role | Recommended daily intake |
|---|---|---|
| Biotin | Keratin production | 30 µg |
| Zinc | Hair follicle cell division | 8-10 mg |
| Iron | Oxygen supply to follicles | 15-18 mg |

### A study base with some limitations

> "The benefit of supplementation shows up mainly in cases of a diagnosed deficiency, less so when intake is already sufficient."
> — Cochrane Review, nutrition and hair health

A balanced intake therefore remains more important than high single doses without a confirmed deficiency.

## Practical steps for daily life

1. Have iron and vitamin D levels checked by a doctor
2. Ensure a protein-rich diet with enough zinc and biotin
3. Make stress management a consistent part of the routine

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why do women lose more hair after a certain age?</summary>

Hair loss in women increases mainly after menopause, when estrogen levels drop and the ratio to androgens shifts. Studies show up to 40% of women experience diffuse hair thinning after menopause. Iron deficiency, thyroid disorders and chronic stress further amplify the effect.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which nutrients are proven to help against hair loss?</summary>

Biotin, zinc, iron and vitamin D show a measurable link with hair health in studies, especially in cases of a diagnosed deficiency. Adequate protein intake also matters, since hair is about 95% keratin.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does it take to see improvement?</summary>

The hair growth cycle lasts 2 to 6 years per strand, which is why visible improvements from supplementation or dietary changes usually take 3 to 6 months of consistent use to appear.

</details>
